# Spanish for English speakers · Mastery plan (100 lessons)

Lingua Onde's own course from C1 to C2 (C1 1–25, C2 26–100, as in
content/FORMAT.md, which the checker enforces, and as in the Dutch, English and
Vietnamese Mastery), built on the Perfectionnement shape (see
`work/PATTERNS.md` and METHOD.md section 3). The Spanish of Spain as Madrid
speaks and writes it today (distinción, vosotros, the perfect for today, coche,
ordenador, móvil, zumo), spelled by the RAE; the other varieties only where a
lesson is about them, and then named; everything else in British English (you
to the learner). Read `work/BRIEF.md`, section "Mastery", first, then the
writer brief `work/es/en/mastery-writer.md`.

How the course works:
- Each lesson is a long text complete in itself, in an authentic style: 25–40
  numbered sentences, 350–700 words, at natural speed. The kind changes
  every day; humour and irony run through it. All texts are original;
  literary pieces are written "in the manner of" an era or genre, never
  imitating a living author's text. Real people, places and history: only
  well-established facts (the facts in this plan are checked before writing
  starts: keep them); quote only titles or a few words of public-domain works;
  modern authors appear by name and title only. No continuing story, no
  recurring cast: a name belongs to one lesson (the revision text reuses the
  week's people).
- Each week has a focus of style (below); its six texts practise it and the
  revision lesson puts it in order.
- Notes 10–18: register and its shifts (tú and usted, the noun style, the
  colloquial markers), what is meant but not said, irony, cultural and
  historical references, idioms and collocations, the precise word and its
  near-synonyms, rhetoric; contrasts with English. Foundation and
  Perfectionnement taught the grammar (the Foundation plan and the
  Perfectionnement plan name each point): point back to them, never teach
  them again.
- Exercise 1: five demanding sentences or short passages to translate, where
  the nuance matters. Exercise 2: five collocations, idioms or connectors to
  fill in.
- Open tasks (`tasks:`): one or two per lesson, named in the plan line. Each
  has the instruction, a model answer in Spanish and a self-check list
  (meaning, register, structure, idiom, effect).
- Revision lessons (7, 14 ... 98): a revision text (20–30 sentences, refs to
  the week's lessons) and notes that put the week's style in order; no
  exercises; one task.
- From lesson 50 the Mastery wave: the learner recreates the old text from
  the English with the same meaning, tone and effect, then compares.
- Perfectionnement's active wave goes on alongside: for a learner who
  finished it, Mastery's first lessons each bring back one of its last
  lessons (the app does it). The introduction says so in a sentence.
- What the learner already has, and Mastery does not repeat: Foundation's
  scenes and texts (breakfast at the bar, churros at San Ginés, the metro,
  the Retiro, the Prado, the Alhambra, Toledo, the tortilla and its onion,
  El Gordo, the Camino de Santiago, Las Meninas, the Fallas, San Fermín, the
  siesta and Spain's clock, the residents' meeting and its minutes, the
  emptied villages, paella's rules, the languages of Spain, the complaint
  letter and the hoja de reclamaciones, que te mejores, Spanish in the world,
  carro and celular, the Andalusian accent and the Canarian guagua,
  diminutives and ahorita, the idioms of lesson 87, the proverbs, the false
  friends of lesson 96, Don Quijote); and Perfectionnement's word lessons,
  portraits and figures as its plan (`work/es/en/perfectionnement.md`) names
  them. A Mastery lesson may glance back at them in a note; it never retells
  them. Before writing starts, the reviewer checks this plan against the
  Perfectionnement plan and replaces any figure or place both courses
  portray.

The book around the lessons, as for Perfectionnement: an introduction
(`content/es/appendix-mastery.en.txt`, `== intro`: what the course is for,
how a lesson and its tasks work, the two waves, the sentence about
Perfectionnement's wave above), written with lesson 1; and after lesson 100
a style and grammar appendix and an index (topic → lessons), plus the
expressions. Register the file in `src/appendix.rs` (BUILTIN).
