# Dutch for Italian speakers · Mastery plan (100 lessons)

Lingua Onde's own course from C1 to C2 (C1 1–25, C2 26–100, as in
content/FORMAT.md, which the checker enforces, and as in the English and
Vietnamese Mastery), built on the Perfectionnement shape (see
`work/PATTERNS.md` and METHOD.md section 3). Standard Netherlands Dutch (the
words, forms and spelling of the Netherlands, the Groene Boekje); Flemish
only where a lesson is about Flanders, and then named as Flemish;
everything else in Italian (tu to the learner). Read `work/BRIEF.md`,
section "Mastery", first, then the writer brief
`work/nl/it/mastery-writer.md`.

How the course works:
- Each lesson is a long text complete in itself, in an authentic style: 25–40
  numbered sentences, 350–700 words, at natural speed. The kind changes
  every day; humour and irony run through it. All texts are original;
  literary pieces are written "in the manner of" an era or genre, never
  imitating a living author's text. Real people, places and history: only
  well-established facts (the facts in this plan were checked: keep them);
  quote only titles or a few words of public-domain works; modern authors
  appear by name and title only. No continuing story, no recurring cast: a
  name belongs to one lesson (the revision text reuses the week's people).
- Each week has a focus of style (below); its six texts practise it and the
  revision lesson puts it in order.
- Notes 10–18: register and its shifts (je and u, the noun style, the
  particles), what is meant but not said, irony, cultural and historical
  references, idioms and collocations, the precise word and its
  near-synonyms, rhetoric; contrasts with Italian. Foundation and
  Perfectionnement taught the grammar (the Foundation plan and the
  Perfectionnement plan name each point): point back to them, never teach
  them again.
- Exercise 1: five demanding sentences or short passages to translate, where
  the nuance matters. Exercise 2: five collocations, idioms or connectors to
  fill in.
- Open tasks (`tasks:`): one or two per lesson, named in the plan line. Each
  has the instruction, a model answer and a self-check list (meaning,
  register, structure, idiom, effect).
- Revision lessons (7, 14 ... 98): a revision text (20–30 sentences, refs to
  the week's lessons) and notes that put the week's style in order; no
  exercises; one task.
- From lesson 50 the Mastery wave: the learner recreates the old text from
  the Italian with the same meaning, tone and effect, then compares.
- Perfectionnement's active wave goes on alongside: for a learner who
  finished it, Mastery's first lessons each bring back one of its last
  lessons (the app does it). The introduction says so in a sentence.
- What the learner already has, and Mastery does not repeat: Foundation's
  scenes and texts (the birthday circle, the herring, Maastricht, the
  Nachtwacht, the polder, Koningsdag, Sinterklaas, Fietsland, the
  Afsluitdijk, the complaint letter, directness "Dat is niet goed",
  everyday idioms, false friends, Dutch in Belgium, the kingdom's
  languages); Perfectionnement's word lessons (zitten, houden, lopen,
  zetten, halen, hand, staan, trekken, water), its portraits (Zeeland,
  Groningen, Antwerpen, Leiden, Den Haag, Suriname, Gent, the Wadden,
  Afrikaans, Rotterdam), its figures (Vermeer, Mondriaan, Escher, Annie
  M.G. Schmidt, Anne Frank, Spinoza, Erasmus, Van Gogh, Multatuli in the
  book club), verzuiling, the news bulletin and headlines, the bezwaarschrift
  and officialese, Dutch-English office jargon, the spelling of the tussen-n
  and -d/-t, proverbs and animal idioms. A Mastery lesson may glance back at
  them in a note; it never retells them.
