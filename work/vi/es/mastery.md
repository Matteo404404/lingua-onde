# Vietnamese for Spanish speakers · Mastery plan (100 lessons)

Lingua Onde's own course from C1 to C2 (C1 1–25, C2 26–100), built on the
Perfectionnement shape (see `work/PATTERNS.md` and METHOD.md section 3).
Northern Vietnamese as the standard (other varieties where a lesson is about
them); everything else in Spanish (neutral, tú to the learner). Read
`work/BRIEF.md`, section "Mastery", first.

How the course works:
- Each lesson is a long text complete in itself, in an authentic style: 25–40
  numbered sentences, about 450–900 syllables, at natural speed. The kind
  changes every day; humour and irony run through it. All texts are
  original; literary pieces are written "in the manner of" an era or genre,
  never imitating a living author's text. Real people, places and history:
  only well-established facts; quote only titles or a few words of
  public-domain works (ca dao, tục ngữ, Truyện Kiều, classical texts).
- Each week has a focus of style (below); its six texts practise it and the
  revision lesson puts it in order.
- Notes 10–18: register and its shifts, xưng hô and what it signals, what is
  meant but not said, irony, cultural and historical references, thành ngữ
  and collocations, Hán Việt against native words, the precise word and its
  near-synonyms, rhetoric; contrasts with Spanish.
- Exercise 1: five demanding sentences or short passages to translate, where
  the nuance matters. Exercise 2: five collocations, idioms or connectors to
  fill in.
- Open tasks (`tasks:`): one or two per lesson, named in the plan line. Each
  has the instruction, a model answer and a self-check list (meaning,
  register, structure, idiom, effect).
- Revision lessons (7, 14 ... 98): a revision text (20–30 sentences, refs to
  the week's lessons) and notes that put the week's style in order; no
  exercises; one task.
- From lesson 50 the Mastery wave: the learner recreates the old text from
  the Spanish with the same meaning, tone and effect, then compares.
- Perfectionnement's active wave goes on alongside: for a learner who
  finished it, Mastery's first lessons each bring back one of its last
  lessons (the app does it). The introduction says so in a sentence.

The book around the lessons, as for Perfectionnement: an introduction
(`content/vi/appendix-mastery.es.txt`, `== intro`: what the course is for,
how a lesson and its tasks work, the two waves, the sentence about
Perfectionnement's wave above); and after lesson 100 a style and grammar
appendix and an index (topic → lessons), plus the expressions. Register the
file in `src/appendix.rs` (BUILTIN).
