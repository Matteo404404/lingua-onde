# Vietnamese for Spanish speakers · Perfectionnement plan (70 lessons)

For learners who have finished Foundation (B2 at the start, C1 at the end).
Northern Vietnamese (Hanoi); everything else in Spanish (neutral, tú to the
learner). Levels: B2 1–45, C1 46–70. Read `work/PATTERNS.md` (section
"Perfectionnement") and `work/BRIEF.md` (section "Perfectionnement") first.
The model is Assimil's Perfectionnement (the Spanish and German volumes);
there is no Vietnamese one, so the frame is theirs and the content is
Vietnam's.

How the course works:
- Each lesson is a text complete in itself; the kind changes from day to
  day. No growth in length: 14–25 numbered sentences or lines, about
  250–550 syllables.
- Humour is the second aim: wordplay on the lesson's keyword, satire, an
  absurd last line. Informative texts may end plainly or with a wink.
- A "word" lesson about every eight lessons: one word and its idioms
  (ăn, chơi, mặt, đi, lòng, bụng, làm, ra, nói); nearly every note is an
  idiom (15–30 notes) and the notes end with the expression list (each
  idiom with its sentence and note number).
- A portrait of a place about every seven lessons, on one template: where
  and what it is like, its towns or quarters, a remark of history, the
  landscape, what is made there, food and drink, a last line on a
  speciality.
- Figures of the past and artists: only well-established facts (dates,
  works, what every Vietnamese schoolbook says); legends told as legends
  ("tương truyền"). Quote only titles or a few words of old public-domain
  works (Truyện Kiều, ca dao, tục ngữ).
- Notes 8–15 (more in word lessons): word families and senses, thành ngữ
  and tục ngữ with their literal sense first, grammar the learner "knows
  but uses badly" (thì, mà, cũng, lại, được, cho, đâu, chứ, nữa / thêm,
  vẫn / còn, mỗi / từng, các / những), register, xưng hô, Sino-Vietnamese
  (Hán Việt) words and their native doublets; "recuerda" back references.
- Exercises: 5 + 5 (translate; fill in), sentences of 5–20 words.
- No figured pronunciation and no culture section: culture lives in the
  texts and the notes. A sound is noted only where it matters (a pun, a
  regional form), with the key's figures (content/vi/pronunciation.md).
- Revision lessons (7, 14 ... 70) have no text: numbered sections on finer
  points, with tables and examples from the week, then a short practice of
  five + five items (in ex1 and ex2).
- From lesson 50 the active wave brings back lesson n − 49.
- Foundation's active wave goes on alongside: for a learner who finished
  Foundation, lessons 1–49 each bring back one of Foundation 52–100 (the app
  does it). Lesson 2 says so in a sentence, and says that a learner who
  starts here directly simply begins the course's own wave at lesson 50.

## The book around the lessons

As in the Assimil Perfectionnement volumes, after lesson 70 and before the
release: `content/vi/appendix-perfectionnement.es.txt` with the
introduction (`== intro`), the grammar appendix (`== grammar`, the finer
points of the revision lessons, with tables), the grammar index (`== index`,
topic → lessons) and the expressions of the word lessons (`== phrases`).

## Lessons

## Week 1

### 1 · B2 · word · Ăn
- A humorous reflection on ăn, the verb that does far more than eat: ăn
  cơm (have a meal; "Ăn cơm chưa?" as a greeting), ăn mặc, ăn nói, ăn ảnh,
  ăn ý, ăn khách, ăn mừng, ăn Tết, ăn cưới, ăn hỏi, ăn vặt, ăn gian, ăn
  bám, ăn theo, ăn không ngồi rồi, and the proverb ăn trông nồi, ngồi
  trông hướng. Told through one man, ông Tư, over one day. Last line: a
  neighbour asks him "Ông ăn cơm chưa?", and ông Tư, who has done all of
  the above, has not yet eaten.
- Focus: ăn in fixed pairs (ăn + verb, ăn + noun); "Ăn cơm chưa?" as a
  greeting (Fundamentos, the lesson that has it).

### 2 · B2 · introduction · Những gì đang chờ bạn
- The course presents itself: a tour of Vietnam in texts of every kind,
  from a legend to a letter to the ward office, a portrait of Huế, a market
  quarrel, a meeting of the street committee. Foundation's wave goes on
  (one sentence); a learner who starts here begins this course's wave at
  lesson 50. Ends with a wink: by lesson 70 you will understand why
  Vietnamese say "không sao" when something is wrong.
- Focus: sẽ + verb + xong / được (there is no future perfect: đến cuối khóa
  bạn sẽ đọc xong …); để and nhằm (purpose, first look at nhằm).

### 3 · B2 · dialogue · Ra mắt
- A young man (Huy) meets his girlfriend's parents for the first time.
  He calls the father "anh" by mistake, then "chú", then "bác"; he says
  "cảm ơn" too often; the mother keeps filling his bowl ("ăn đi cháu").
  Last line: he finally says "Con cảm ơn bố ạ", and the father answers:
  "Gọi bố sớm thế? Chưa ăn hỏi đâu nhé."
- Focus: xưng hô with a partner's parents (bác / cháu, then con / bố mẹ);
  ạ and thưa; gắp thức ăn cho khách; đâu as negation (chưa … đâu).

### 4 · B2 · portrait · Phố cổ Hà Nội
- The template: the Old Quarter north of Hoàn Kiếm lake; streets named
  after what was sold there (Hàng Bạc, Hàng Đường, Hàng Mã…, "Hà Nội ba
  mươi sáu phố phường" though there are more); tube houses (nhà ống),
  narrow and deep; Đồng Xuân market; the morning on small plastic stools;
  what is made and sold now (paper offerings on Hàng Mã, silver); last
  line: cà phê trứng, born in Hanoi in the 1940s, more dessert than coffee.
- Focus: relative mà and nơi; được gọi là / mang tên; hàng (shop, goods,
  street name).

### 5 · B2 · story · Ông khách quen đường
- A xe ôm driver picks up a foreigner at the station and takes the long
  way round, explaining every corner as to a tourist. The passenger listens
  politely, pays, and at the end says in perfect Vietnamese that he has
  lived in this street for ten years, and shows him the short way back.
- Focus: narrative markers bèn, liền, thì ra / hóa ra; đã … thì; the past
  from context and đã; tưởng (wrongly believe).

### 6 · B2 · anecdotes · Giờ cao su
- Three short original anecdotes about "rubber time" (giờ cao su), each
  with a twist: a wedding announced for 11 where the guests come at 12; a
  meeting where the only punctual one is the foreigner; a man who is early
  once in his life, on the wrong day.
- Focus: vừa … đã, mới … đã, chưa … đã ("apenas … cuando"); giờ cao su,
  đúng giờ, trễ / muộn.

### 7 · revision
- Sections: 1 xưng hô with a partner's family: bác / cháu, bố / con,
  thưa … ạ (table of who says what to whom); 2 the ăn family (table:
  ăn + noun, ăn + verb, meaning, sentence); 3 narrative markers bèn, liền,
  thì ra, hóa ra, tưởng (table with examples from lessons 3–6); 4 vừa / mới
  / chưa … đã; 5 the future done: sẽ … xong / được / rồi, để vs nhằm.
  Practice.

## Week 2

### 8 · B2 · sketch · Chú, bác, cô, dì
- A Spanish husband at his Vietnamese wife's family reunion, with a
  notebook: in the North the elder siblings of either parent are bác; the
  father's younger brother chú and chú's wife thím, the father's younger
  sister cô, the mother's younger brother cậu and his wife mợ, the
  mother's younger sister dì, the husbands of cô and dì dượng;
  ông bà nội on one side, ông bà ngoại on the other. He gets everyone right
  by dinner. Last line: a five-year-old cousin points at him and says
  "Chú Tây", and the whole table agrees.
- Focus: northern kinship terms (paternal vs maternal); nội / ngoại; Tây
  as "Westerner" (friendly, informal); northern bác for elder siblings of
  either parent, with a note that the South differs.

### 9 · B2 · word · Chơi
- A humorous reflection on chơi: đi chơi, chơi đàn, chơi thể thao, chơi
  với ai, ăn chơi, chơi chữ, chơi khăm, chơi xấu, chơi đẹp, chơi trội,
  chơi dại, nói chơi, chơi vơi … Told through a grandmother who has never
  "played" in her life and a grandson who does nothing else. Last line:
  she is the one with the best chơi chữ at Tết.
- Focus: chơi as play / visit / keep company / do for fun; the expression
  list.

### 10 · B2 · satire · Họp
- The art of the meeting at a Vietnamese office: the meeting to prepare
  the meeting, the opening speech, "ý kiến của các đồng chí", the
  conclusion that a further meeting is needed. Last line: the next meeting
  will discuss how to have fewer meetings.
- Focus: nominalisers việc, cuộc, sự (Fundamentos, the lesson that
  explained them) in depth; meeting words: chủ trì, thống nhất, triển khai,
  ý kiến, kết luận.

### 11 · B2 · portrait · Huế
- The template: the centre of the country on the Hương river; the citadel
  and the tombs of the Nguyễn kings, capital from 1802 to 1945; a slow,
  quiet city, rain and the purple áo dài of schoolgirls; conical hats (nón
  lá); royal and street cuisine, bún bò Huế, the many small chè; last line:
  the soft Huế speech, mô, tê, răng, rứa (đâu, kia, sao, thế).
- Focus: Hán Việt names (kinh thành, lăng, hoàng đế); comparison with
  Hanoi speech; từng (once, used to).

### 12 · B2 · dialogue · Thuê nhà
- A young woman negotiates the rent of a small flat with the landlady:
  price, deposit (tiền cọc), electricity and water paid apart, the motorbike
  in the hall. Each problem the landlady turns into an advantage. Last
  line: the landlady lives next door and "drops by" every day; that is the
  real price.
- Focus: softeners: thôi thì, hay là, cũng được, để … xem đã, chứ; bớt
  (lower the price), cọc, điện nước.

### 13 · B2 · reflection · Cảm ơn
- Why Vietnamese say cảm ơn and xin lỗi less than Spanish speakers inside
  the family and more with strangers; a son who thanks his mother for
  dinner and she asks what he has done wrong; làm phiền, ngại quá, không
  dám (when thanked by an elder), phiền anh. Last line: the author thanks
  the reader, and hopes it does not sound too distant.
- Focus: thanks and apologies by relationship; không dám, ngại; khách sáo.

### 14 · revision
- Sections: 1 kinship terms, the full table (paternal / maternal, their
  spouses, nội / ngoại), with what you call them and what they call you;
  2 the chơi family; 3 nominalisers sự, việc, cuộc (which goes with what,
  table); 4 softeners in a negotiation; 5 thanks and apologies by
  relationship (table). Practice.

## Week 3

### 15 · B2 · word · Mặt
- A reflection on mặt: mặt trời, mặt trăng, mặt mũi, mặt hàng, mặt đường,
  trước mặt, ra mặt, mất mặt, giữ mặt mũi / thể diện, mặt dày, mặt nặng
  mày nhẹ, mặt cắt không còn giọt máu, mặt trái, có mặt, vắng mặt. Told
  through a class photo day. Last line: the one pupil absent (vắng mặt)
  is the only one whose face is on the cover.
- Focus: mặt as face / surface / side / presence; the expression list.

### 16 · B2 · figure · Nguyễn Du
- Nguyễn Du (1765–1820) and Truyện Kiều: 3,254 verses in lục bát written
  in chữ Nôm, from a Chinese novel; the story of Thúy Kiều in one sentence;
  why every Vietnamese knows lines of it; "bói Kiều" (opening the book at
  random to read your fate). Quote at most the first verse ("Trăm năm trong
  cõi người ta"). Last line: a grandmother who cannot read but recites a
  hundred lines.
- Focus: lục bát (six and eight syllables) first look; Hán Việt in
  literature (tác phẩm, tác giả, thi hào); thuộc lòng.

### 17 · B2 · anecdote · Bà gọi video
- A grandmother learns video calls: she holds the phone to her ear, calls
  at five in the morning, shows the ceiling, and talks to everyone at
  once. Twist: by the end of the month she has a hundred followers for her
  cooking videos and her grandson has to book a call with her.
- Focus: bảo, dặn, nhắc (tell, instruct, remind); từ láy describing
  manner (lóng ngóng, loay hoay, cuống quýt).

### 18 · B2 · portrait · Hội An
- The template: the old trading port on the Thu Bồn river, Japanese and
  Chinese merchants in the 16th–19th centuries; the covered bridge (Chùa
  Cầu); yellow walls and lanterns, the full-moon nights; tailors who sew a
  suit in a day; the river and the floods; cao lầu and the street food;
  last line: you come for a day and leave with three suits.
- Focus: từng, xưa kia, ngày nay; được + verb (passive of praise), nổi
  tiếng với / về.

### 19 · B2 · news · Bản tin tối (1)
- An evening news bulletin, invented and plausible: a new bus line in the
  city, a rice harvest in the Red River delta, a school opening, a
  traffic jam after rain. No real events or names.
- Focus: news register: theo, cho biết, dự kiến, khoảng, tăng / giảm,
  tỷ lệ, chiếm; numbers in news (phần trăm, nghìn tỷ đồng).

### 20 · B2 · news · Bản tin tối (2)
- The second half: sport (a football match lost in the last minute),
  culture (a festival), and the weather: the northeast monsoon (gió mùa
  đông bắc), rét đậm, fog in the mountains. Last line: the newsreader's
  "Chúc quý vị buổi tối vui vẻ" while it rains in the studio window.
- Focus: weather register; quý vị; dự báo, có khả năng, kèm theo.

### 21 · revision
- Sections: 1 the mặt family; 2 lục bát: the count and the rhyme (6th
  syllable of the six with the 6th of the eight), as a reading aid only;
  3 news register (table: everyday word → news word: nói → cho biết, khoảng
  → ước tính, muốn → dự kiến); 4 từ láy of manner; 5 bảo, dặn, nhắc, khuyên.
  Practice.

## Week 4

### 22 · B2 · dialogue · Phong bì
- A couple decide how much money to put in the envelope for a wedding: it
  depends on how close they are, whether both go, how expensive the
  restaurant is, how much the couple gave at theirs. Last line: after half
  an hour they find out the wedding was yesterday.
- Focus: tùy, tùy theo, tính (calculate / reckon), ít ra, chí ít, vừa
  phải; đi đám cưới, mừng cưới.

### 23 · B2 · art · Tranh Đông Hồ
- Đông Hồ woodblock prints from Bắc Ninh: paper made with powdered shells
  (giấy điệp), natural colours, pictures bought for Tết; famous subjects:
  the pig with her piglets, the mouse wedding (đám cưới chuột, with the
  cat who must be paid). Last line: the cat in the print still takes its
  share, as cats do.
- Focus: describing a picture (bức, tấm, nét, màu); tượng trưng cho; the
  classifiers bức / tấm / tờ.

### 24 · B2 · portrait · Sài Gòn
- The template: the biggest city, in the south; renamed Thành phố Hồ Chí
  Minh in 1976 and still called Sài Gòn by everyone; Bến Thành market,
  Chợ Lớn; the pace, the pavement coffee; the southern speech (nha, nè,
  hông, vô); cơm tấm and broken rice; last line: in the south the iced
  coffee is sweeter, and the people say so is the welcome.
- Focus: Southern forms against Hanoi forms (table in the notes); nhộn
  nhịp, sầm uất.

### 25 · B2 · word · Đi
- A reflection on đi: đi làm, đi học, đi chợ, đi vắng, đi bộ, đi đường,
  đi tắt, đi đêm, đi lại, đi đôi với, đi guốc trong bụng, đi đời, đi xa
  (euphemism for dying), and final đi in orders. Told through a man who
  goes everywhere and arrives nowhere. Last line: his wife's "Đi đi!"
- Focus: đi as go / travel / wear (đi giày) / final particle; the
  expression list.

### 26 · B2 · satire · Con nhà người ta
- "The neighbours' child": the perfect child every parent quotes, first in
  class, plays the piano, helps at home, never on the phone. Last line: the
  neighbours' child's mother is heard telling him about "con nhà người ta"
  too: the narrator.
- Focus: so sánh: hơn, kém, bằng, chẳng bằng; mà xem (look at …); người ta
  as "others".

### 27 · B2 · legend · Sơn Tinh, Thủy Tinh
- The legend told as a legend: the Hùng king's daughter Mị Nương, two
  suitors, the mountain spirit and the water spirit; the strange wedding
  gifts (voi chín ngà, gà chín cựa, ngựa chín hồng mao); Sơn Tinh arrives
  first; Thủy Tinh's anger, the floods every year. Last line: so every
  summer the water still comes for the bride.
- Focus: ngày xửa ngày xưa, tương truyền, bèn, liền, từ đó; giành, đánh
  nhau; the old storyteller's style.

### 28 · revision
- Sections: 1 the đi family; 2 legend style (table: storyteller words and
  their everyday equivalents); 3 comparison in all its forms (hơn, kém,
  bằng, nhất, chẳng bằng, càng … càng); 4 classifiers for pictures and
  paper (bức, tấm, tờ, cuốn / quyển); 5 Hanoi and Saigon forms (table).
  Practice.

## Week 5

### 29 · B2 · dialogue · Xe cũ
- Buying a used motorbike from a smooth seller: "chính chủ", "còn mới
  tinh", "đi êm như ru", "chỉ chở bà đi chợ". The buyer asks to try it; it
  starts at the fourth kick. Last line: the seller's grandmother rides past
  on a new one.
- Focus: persuading and promising: đảm bảo, cam đoan, chắc chắn, thề;
  similes with như (êm như ru).

### 30 · B2 · portrait · Sa Pa
- The template: the north-west mountains near the Chinese border; Fansipan,
  more than 3,100 metres, the highest peak of Indochina; terraced rice
  fields; the H'Mông and Red Dao peoples and their markets; fog and cold;
  embroidery and indigo cloth; corn wine and grilled food in the evening;
  last line: in Sa Pa you can see four seasons in one day.
- Focus: geography words (đỉnh, dãy núi, thung lũng, ruộng bậc thang);
  dân tộc thiểu số; được mệnh danh là.

### 31 · B2 · character · Bà bán xôi đầu ngõ
- The sticky-rice seller at the end of the lane, who knows everything about
  everyone: who came home late, who argued, who is pregnant before the
  mother knows. Last line: the only thing nobody knows is her own name.
- Focus: hễ … là, bao giờ cũng, chẳng … bao giờ; từ láy of character
  (lanh lợi, tò mò, xởi lởi).

### 32 · B2 · figure · Hồ Xuân Hương
- The poet of the late 18th and early 19th century, called "bà chúa thơ
  Nôm": poems in chữ Nôm, sharp and witty, often with two meanings; her
  life known only in part (say so). Name one poem by its title (Bánh trôi
  nước) and say what it is about, without quoting it. Last line: two
  centuries later, pupils still blush and laugh at the same lines.
- Focus: chữ Nôm against chữ quốc ngữ, first look; lời ít ý nhiều;
  nghĩa đen / nghĩa bóng.

### 33 · B2 · word · Lòng
- A reflection on lòng: lòng tốt, vui lòng, hài lòng, bằng lòng, mất lòng,
  yên lòng, an lòng, nằm lòng, thuộc lòng, lòng bàn tay, đau lòng, một
  lòng, lòng lợn (the dish), "mất lòng trước, được lòng sau". Told through
  a restaurant owner. Last line: the most satisfied customer (hài lòng)
  ordered lòng lợn.
- Focus: lòng as heart / feeling / inside, and as tripe; the expression
  list.

### 34 · B2 · poem · Mưa phùn
- An original poem in lục bát, eight to twelve lines, on the drizzle of
  late winter in Hanoi: wet streets, a vendor, damp walls, waiting for
  spring. Each six-line and eight-line must count and rhyme correctly
  (6th of the six with the 6th of the eight; 8th of the eight with the 6th
  of the next six). Then a short prose line to close.
- Focus: lục bát counting and rhyme; từ láy in poetry (lất phất, lành
  lạnh, mịt mờ).

### 35 · revision
- Sections: 1 the lòng family; 2 lục bát rules (table: syllable positions,
  bằng / trắc, rhyme), with the poem of lesson 34; 3 persuasion and
  promises; 4 hễ … là, bao giờ cũng; 5 nghĩa đen / nghĩa bóng; chữ Nôm and
  quốc ngữ. Practice.

## Week 6

### 36 · B2 · essay · Thể diện
- Face: why a "no" is rarely said, "để em xem đã", "cũng được", "không
  sao"; giving face at a dinner (who pays), losing face in public, nể.
  Last line: the end of lesson 2's promise: "không sao" said when
  everything is wrong.
- Focus: indirect refusal; nể, ngại, giữ thể diện, mất mặt (lesson 15).

### 37 · B2 · portrait · Miền Tây
- The template: the Mekong Delta (đồng bằng sông Cửu Long, the "nine
  dragons"); Cần Thơ and the floating market of Cái Răng; canals and boats;
  rice and orchards; đờn ca tài tử music; fish and fruit; last line: you
  ask for directions and get them by boat.
- Focus: water words (kênh, rạch, ghe, xuồng); southern words met in
  lesson 24.

### 38 · B2 · dialogue · Cãi nhau ở chợ
- Two sellers at the market fight politely and then less politely over one
  customer ("Chị nói thế mà nghe được à?", "Ai mời khách trước?"). The
  customer, meanwhile, buys from a third seller and leaves.
- Focus: rhetorical questions (Fundamentos, the lesson that explained ai
  chẳng, đâu phải, chẳng lẽ) in a quarrel; mà nghe được à, có … đâu.

### 39 · B2 · history · Hai Bà Trưng
- The Trưng sisters: the uprising of the year 40 against the Han governor,
  their rule from Mê Linh, the defeat in 43; temples, a district of Hanoi
  and streets named after them; told with "tương truyền" where history
  becomes legend. Plain ending.
- Focus: historical narration; khởi nghĩa, chống, đánh đuổi; Hán Việt
  titles; được ghi nhớ.

### 40 · B2 · word · Bụng
- A reflection on bụng: đau bụng, đói bụng, no bụng, tốt bụng, xấu bụng,
  bụng bảo dạ, mở cờ trong bụng, có bụng, đi guốc trong bụng (lesson 25),
  "no bụng đói con mắt". Told through a family dinner. Last line: the
  kindest man (tốt bụng) is the one who always has room for dessert.
- Focus: bụng as belly / heart / intention; the expression list.

### 41 · B2 · jokes · Ba chuyện cười
- Three short original jokes: a stingy man (keo kiệt), a teacher and a
  lazy pupil, a husband and the electricity bill. Each with a clean
  punchline that works in Vietnamese.
- Focus: how a joke is told (một hôm, … bèn hỏi, … đáp); keo kiệt, ki bo.

## Week 7

### 43 · B2 · portrait · Đà Lạt
- The template: the Central Highlands at about 1,500 metres; a hill

### 44 · B2 · legend · Bánh chưng, bánh giầy
- The legend of Lang Liêu: the Hùng king asks his sons for the best dish

- Focus: legend style (lesson 27); tượng trưng cho; tròn / vuông; trời /

### 45 · B2 · word · Làm

- Focus: làm as do / make / work / act as / cause; làm cho, khiến; the
  expression list.

### 46 · C1 · dialogue · Họp tổ dân phố

  register. Last line: the karaoke comes from the chairman's own house.
- Focus: speech formulas: kính thưa, thứ nhất, thứ hai, tóm lại, đề

### 47 · C1 · letter · Đơn kiến nghị (1)

### 48 · C1 · letter · Thư trả lời (2)

## Week 8

### 50 · C1 · portrait · Ninh Bình
- The template: south of the Red River delta; Hoa Lư, the capital before

  goat and crispy rice (cơm cháy); last line: Hạ Long on land, as the

- Focus: Hán Việt place words (cố đô, kinh đô); được ví như.

### 51 · C1 · figure · Nguyễn Trãi
- Nguyễn Trãi (1380–1442): adviser of Lê Lợi in the war against the Ming;
  the proclamation Bình Ngô đại cáo (1428) after the victory; scholar and

  rehabilitation later under Lê Thánh Tông. Plain, serious ending.
- Focus: historical narration in C1 register; Hán Việt abstract words
  (nhân nghĩa, an dân); bị + verb in history.

### 52 · C1 · satire · Báo cáo thành tích
- The year-end achievement report: everything "cơ bản hoàn thành", "đạt
  và vượt chỉ tiêu", "tích cực", "đồng bộ", "quyết liệt", lessons "rút kinh
  nghiệm sâu sắc". Last line: the report on how the report was written.
- Focus: officialese and its Hán Việt compounds; set phrases of reports;
  irony through register.

### 53 · C1 · word · Ra
- A reflection on ra: ra đời, ra mắt (lesson 3), ra vẻ, ra dáng, ra trò,
  ra gì, ra sao, hóa ra, nghĩ ra, nhận ra, tìm ra, ra tay, ra lệnh, ra

  the book finally ra đời, nobody nhận ra the author.
- Focus: ra as directional and as result (nghĩ ra, nhận ra); the
  expression list.

### 54 · C1 · dialogue · Ngày giỗ
- A death-anniversary meal (giỗ) at the eldest uncle's house: the incense,
  the dishes on the altar, who sits where, the polite words for death (mất,
  qua đời, đi xa, khuất núi); two cousins argue over whose turn it is to

- Focus: euphemisms for death; respectful register to elders; cúng, thắp

### 55 · C1 · portrait · Người Việt ở nước ngoài

## Week 9

### 57 · C1 · proverbs · Uống nước nhớ nguồn
- A father talks to his student son only in tục ngữ (uống nước nhớ nguồn,
  có công mài sắt có ngày nên kim, một cây làm chẳng nên non…, gần mực thì

- Focus: phòng khi, lỡ, kẻo, nhỡ đâu; tiết kiệm, hà tiện.

### 59 · C1 · history · Ba thứ chữ
- Three scripts: Chinese characters (chữ Hán) for centuries of learning and
  administration; chữ Nôm for Vietnamese itself (Truyện Kiều, Hồ Xuân
  Hương); the Latin script, chữ quốc ngữ, worked out by Portuguese and other
  missionaries in the 17th century (Alexandre de Rhodes's dictionary,
  Rome 1651), spread in the 20th century and the script of everyone today.
  Plain ending.
- Focus: được phát triển bởi, dần dần, phổ biến, thay thế; the history of
  writing in C1 register.

### 60 · C1 · word · Nói
- A reflection on nói: nói thẳng, nói khéo, nói dối, nói xấu, nói đùa,

  nói một đằng làm một nẻo, ăn nói (lesson 1), nói phải củ cải cũng nghe.
  Told through a politician's campaign day. Last line: the only true
  sentence he says is off the record.
- Focus: nói and its kin (bảo, kể, khuyên, dặn); the expression list.

### 61 · C1 · dialogue · Chuyển nhà

### 62 · C1 · idioms · Thành ngữ con vật

- Focus: four-syllable thành ngữ; như + animal similes; register of
  idioms (friendly, sharp, rude).

### 63 · revision
