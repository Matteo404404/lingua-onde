## The lesson

- `numeral:` the Vietnamese ordinal, as in Le Vietnamien sans peine: Bài thứ
  nhất, Bài thứ hai, Bài thứ ba, Bài thứ tư, Bài thứ năm ... Bài thứ mười,
  Bài thứ mười một ... Bài thứ hai mươi mốt, Bài thứ hai mươi tư ... Bài
  thứ một trăm. `numeral.pron.es:` in lessons 1–14 only.
- `title:` a short Vietnamese title of the scene; `title.es:` its Spanish.
  Revision lessons: title "Ôn tập", title.es "Repaso".
- `dialogue:` one item per line of the plan's size. Each item has `es:` with
  a natural Spanish translation; where Vietnamese builds the sentence
  differently, add the word-for-word sense in brackets, as the Assimil book
  does: «¡Buenos días, señorita! (saludar señorita)». Only where it teaches
  something.
- Address: the plan's kinship words. In Spanish use what a Spanish speaker
  would say in that situation (usted to an older stranger, tú among young
  people or friends) and keep one form per speaker through the lesson; the
  notes explain the Vietnamese word (ông "abuelo", used for "usted, señor").
- `pron.es:` lessons 1–14 only, on every dialogue line: the whole sentence
  figured by the key, one figured word per Vietnamese syllable, separated
  by spaces, each followed by its tone arrow (→ ✓ ↑ ↓), the sentence's
  punctuation after the arrow ("chau→ co→!"). All lowercase. No tone marks
  in the figured words: the arrow carries the tone, è and ò only mark open
  vowels. The app draws high tones on the upper row and low ones below by
  itself. Foreign names stay as written, lowercase, with →: luca→.
  Dialogues of lessons 1–14 write numbers in words, never digits. From
  lesson 15 there is no pron.es at all.
- `sounds.es:` one to three points on a sound or tone of this lesson,
  compared with Spanish, in nearly every lesson of the first weeks, then
  when a sound is new or tricky. Quote figured sounds with the key's signs.
- `notes.es:` four to eight notes on words of the dialogue, in order.
  Explain kinship address, classifiers, particles and time markers simply
  and truly, compared with Spanish ("no hay conjugación: el tiempo lo dicen
  palabras como đã, sẽ"). Point back ("lo vimos en la lección 3") and ahead
  ("lo veremos en la lección de repaso").
- `ex1:` five items to translate, `es:` each; `ex2:` five sentences (four
  in lessons 1–2) with [blanks], `es:` the Spanish prompt, one answer per
  blank. As BRIEF.md says.
- `culture.es:` when the plan names a subject; `aside.es:` when it says so.
- Revision lessons: BRIEF.md "The revision lesson", in Spanish, with the
  plan's sections and full tables (tones with their arrows and rows,
  pronouns by age, classifiers, numbers...). Lessons 7 and 14 have
  pron.es on their dialogue too.

## Rules

- Northern Vietnamese (Hanoi), correct spelling and tone marks, tone marks
  placed in the common print style (hòa, khỏe, thủy, quý). Natural
  Vietnamese as people say it today; nothing a Hanoian would find odd.
- Everything original. Do not open the reference course books (kept outside the repository). Only well-established
  facts about Vietnam.
- Spanish: neutral, tú to the learner ("recuerda", "fíjate"), never
  usted to the learner, no English words in Spanish text.
- Each lesson only uses words and forms met before plus the plan's new
  step; check the lessons before yours.
- Any count a note states is true; every reference points to a lesson that
  really has it; ex2 Spanish is a plain translation, never a riddle or a
  hint about the answer; no line contradicts another.
- Names belong to one lesson; no continuing story.

- Tone names: no mark = ngang, à huyền, á sắc, ả hỏi, ã ngã, ạ nặng. Check
  every tone a sounds line or note names against the mark in the word
  (là and trà are huyền, rộng and học are nặng, cũng is ngã).
- Figured words quoted in sounds or notes follow the key exactly: iê/ia →
  iơ (viên → viơn→), uô/ua → uơ (của → cuơ✓), ay → ăi (máy → măi↑), e is
  always the open è (đẹp → dèp↓, em → èm→), o always the open ò (nhỏ →
  ñò✓, còn → còn→), nh → ñ, k before i/e → qu (kỹ → qui✓). Đẹp has e, not ê.

- ex1 from lesson 8 on: five NEW sentences made of this lesson's words and
  earlier ones, never copies of dialogue lines. Revision lessons have no
  ex1 / ex2.
- Numbers: 14 is mười bốn, 24 hai mươi tư (or hai mươi bốn), 15 mười lăm,
  21 hai mươi mốt; ordinals thứ nhất, thứ hai, thứ ba, thứ tư, then thứ +
  the number (Bài thứ mười bốn).
- Northern words: cốc (not ly), ô (not dù), cân (not ký), bát (not tô),
  bố / mẹ (not ba / má), a southern word only in a note that names it.
- Vâng, dạ and ạ go up the age ladder: an older speaker never says them
  to a younger one.
- Revision dialogues are a small NEW scene of their own, two people talking
  about one situation, never a montage of the week's dialogue lines (see
  035.txt for the model): no names or people
  from earlier lessons (anh ấy, chị ấy instead), one speaker per line,
  speakers alternate, the twist makes sense on its own.
- Spanish spelling: "deme", "dime", "ponme" (no accent).
- In Spanish text dishes keep plain spelling: pho, bún chả, bánh mì.
- Spanish prose stays Spanish: quote a Vietnamese word as an example, never
  use it as a Spanish word ("vuelven a abrir", not "vuelven a mở"; "el
  título", not "el tên phim"). Spanish lines must be grammatical sentences
  ("¡Tu reloj va atrasado!").

## Checks before you finish

- Run `python3 work/tools/figure_vi.py work/vi/es/foundation/NNN.txt` on each
  lesson of 1–14: it rewrites every pron.es line by the key. Then make the
  sounds lines and notes quote the same figures.

- `target/release/marea --check work/vi/es/foundation/NNN.txt` prints
  ok with no problem, for each file. It checks every 🔈 line against the
  tones of its sentence: fix every arrow it reports. A `gap:` line is a
  missing part: add it.
- Reread each lesson: the Vietnamese natural and correct, the twist works,
  the notes true, one answer per blank.
- Touch nothing but your own lesson files. No git, no cargo.

Report briefly: per lesson the lines and new words, the checker output,
anything you were unsure of.
